Why `min(top, left, diagonal)`? A bigger square needs all three sides to be strong enough. If any one neighbor can only support a smaller square, the current square must stop at that smaller size.
Why does the answer become `3`? The final DP value `3` means there is a `3 x 3` square of ones whose bottom-right corner is at that cell.
